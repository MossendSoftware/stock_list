Money supply vs. government debt

Broad money and general-government gross debt, both indexed to 100 (left axis), with debt-to-GDP on the right. When debt outruns money, the gap is funded by real lenders; when money keeps pace, the debt is being monetised.

Govt debt: IMF general government gross debt (% of GDP, FRED GGGDTA*) × local-currency GDP (World Bank). Available for US, UK, Japan, Korea, China, India; Euro-area and Hong Kong series unavailable/insufficient.
