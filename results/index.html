The story

Once you strip out money-supply growth, the emerging-market winners are the biggest losers: the Sensex and Shanghai's entire rise was money printing. Japan, Taiwan and the NASDAQ are the genuine real compounders, and dividends (TR vs price) decide it in Europe.

TR = total return (dividends reinvested); price = price index, which understates the real result by the dividend yield (~2–4%/yr). Money supply: US M2 plus FRED's OECD broad-money series (UK / Euro area / Japan / Korea); China, India, Taiwan and Hong Kong use curated official anchors as FRED's comparable series end in 2018. Figures are approximate; rankings are robust.
